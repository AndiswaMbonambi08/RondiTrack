Yesterdays Question(Purpose of Jobs/)?
runs on its own, on a timer, without anyone clicking anything.
basically like an alarm clock that does chores for you.
CycleCloseJob is the “end of month” chore: Marks that cycle as closed.
                                           Writes down who didn’t pay enough (that’s “arrears”).
                                           Starts the next cycle.

HousekeepingJob is the “clean up” chore. It deletes old tokens and old records that nobody needs anymore.
